# Football Time Machine — Releases

Version themes and intended scope. Treat as a planning map, not a guarantee of ship dates. Align detailed initiatives with [PRODUCT_ROADMAP.md](./PRODUCT_ROADMAP.md) and guardrails in [PRODUCT_PRINCIPLES.md](./PRODUCT_PRINCIPLES.md).

---

## V1.0 — Live

**Theme:** Launch the interactive-documentary core with the first Men’s World Cup shelf.

**Status:** Live

**Includes**
- Live product
- USA 1994
- France 1998
- Korea/Japan 2002
- Story, Essentials, Team Journeys, and Every Match
- Team Profiles
- Canonical editorial content
- Spoiler-safe progression

**Success lens**
Users can start and progress through published tournaments without spoiler leaks, with a coherent experience model across modes.

---

## V1.0.1 — Clarity, watching options, operability, and brand identity

**Theme:** Help new users understand the product, offer highlights alongside full matches, make the live product observable and fixable, and establish a restrained visual identity across site, browser, and sharing surfaces.

**Includes**
- About Page
- Highlights Support
- Feedback System
- Analytics & Monitoring
- Mobile / Accessibility Polish
- Replay Reliability Improvements
- Logo and brand identity
- Favicon and app icons
- Homepage and navigation integration
- Social-sharing brand assets

**Success lens**
First-time visitors understand the spoiler-safe model; users who will not watch a full match can still complete via highlights; actionable feedback and funnel analytics drive what we fix next; the brand mark is recognizable at small sizes, native to the site, and inclusive of future competitions.

---

## V1.1 — Retention, continuity, and documentary framing

**Theme:** Help people come back, keep their place in the story, and enter every tournament as a film—not a fixture list.

**Includes**
- Storyline-Driven Essentials (very high priority)
- Tournament Prologues (high priority)
- Accounts
- Cloud Progress
- Tournament Completion Experience
- Continue Watching improvements
- Team Journey Epilogues

**Success lens**
Essentials reads as narrative chapters built on 4–6 pre-tournament storylines; prologues orient new viewers without spoilers; cross-device resume works; Continue Watching is the natural return path; finishing a tournament feels complete; Team Journey completers get an earned epilogue.

---

## V1.2 — Archive expansion, nation hubs, and discovery

**Theme:** Grow the shelf, deepen discovery, and let nations and cast live beyond a single tournament—without lowering the quality bar.

**Includes**
- National Team Hubs (high priority; expansion of Team Profiles)
- Tournament Cast (medium priority)
- Germany 2006 (full documentary edition: prologue, cast, storyline Essentials, hubs)
- Search
- Player Profiles
- Additional curated journeys

**Success lens**
New editions meet the interactive-documentary quality bar; hubs convert into journey starts across tournaments; cast onboards fans before Match 1; search and profiles stay spoiler-safe; curated journeys show strong completion relative to length.

## Future

Long-horizon themes for the interactive documentary platform—not committed release scope. See Research, Vision, and Strategic Opportunities in the roadmap.

**Includes (illustrative)**
- Women’s World Cups (same prologue / cast / storyline Essentials / hub model)
- Project Cinema (in-product watching research → only if validated)
- Cross-tournament documentaries and Player Journeys
- Other competitions (Euros, Copa América, Champions League campaigns, and more)

**Success lens**
Nothing ships from Future until it answers the roadmap’s three questions and clears product-principle review. Growth means more cinematic editions, not a denser database.

## Release hygiene (lightweight)

- Prefer shipping the MVP named in the roadmap over stretching a version’s theme.
- Historical accuracy and spoiler safety can delay a content release; that is expected.
- Record notable shipped changes here when a version closes; keep planning detail in the roadmap.
- Feed live learnings through [FEEDBACK_LOG.md](./FEEDBACK_LOG.md) and analytics into V1.0.1+ priorities.
- Do not promote Research or Vision items into a release until technical/legal validation and user evidence support it.
